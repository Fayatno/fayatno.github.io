Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Fayatno ("Fayatno", "we", "us", or "our"). Fayatno is a web-based application designed to provide tools for managing Threads content and interactions through authorized integrations with Meta's Threads platform.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be accessed, how it may be used, how it is protected, and the choices available to users of Fayatno.

2. Information We Access

When you connect your Threads account to Fayatno, the application may access information permitted by the permissions you authorize through Meta's authorization system.

Depending on the features you use and the permissions granted, this may include:

  • Basic information associated with your Threads account.
  • Threads content required by application features.
  • Replies and reply-related information required for reply management functionality.
  • Information required to publish or manage content on your behalf after authorization.

3. Threads API Permissions

Fayatno may request the following Threads API permissions:

  • threads_basic — used to access basic Threads account information required by the application.
  • threads_content_publish — used to publish content on behalf of an authorized user.
  • threads_read_replies — used to access replies required by reply-related functionality.
  • threads_manage_replies — used to perform permitted reply-management actions requested by the user.

4. How We Use Information

Information accessed through authorized Threads API permissions is used only to provide the functionality requested by the user.

Depending on the functionality used, information may be used to:

  • Connect and identify the authorized Threads account.
  • Display information required by Fayatno features.
  • Publish authorized content.
  • Retrieve replies when required by the application.
  • Perform reply-management actions requested by the user.
  • Maintain application security and reliability.

5. Information We Do Not Sell

Fayatno does not sell personal information obtained through the application to third parties.

We do not use information obtained through Threads authorization for unrelated purposes.

6. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by Fayatno against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Where authentication credentials or access tokens are processed by the application, they are handled using security measures appropriate to the application's technical environment.

7. Data Retention

Fayatno retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's functionality, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, or resolve legitimate technical issues.

Users may request deletion of information associated with their use of Fayatno by following the instructions on our Data Deletion page .

8. Revoking Access

You may revoke Fayatno's access to your Threads account through the relevant Meta or Threads account controls.

Revoking authorization prevents the application from continuing to access information through the revoked permissions.

9. Third-Party Services

Fayatno uses services provided by Meta, including the Threads API, to provide authorized Threads functionality.

Your use of Threads and Meta services is also subject to the applicable terms and policies provided by Meta.

10. Children's Privacy

Fayatno is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page together with an updated revision date.

12. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, data processing, or deletion requests, please contact us through our Contact page .
