Data Deletion

Information about requesting deletion of data associated with Fayatno.

Requesting Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with their use of Fayatno.

To request deletion, contact us using the contact information provided on our Contact page .

What Happens After a Request?

After receiving a valid deletion request, we will review the request and identify information associated with the applicable user account or authorization.

Information that is no longer required for legitimate operational, security, or legal purposes will be deleted or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

Revoking Threads Access

You may also revoke Fayatno's authorization to access your Threads account through the applicable Meta or Threads account settings.

Need Help?

If you have questions about your deletion request, please contact us through our Contact page .
